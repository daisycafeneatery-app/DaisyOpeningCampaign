Privacy

Privacy Policy

A plain-language overview of how Daisy Cafe & Eatery’s website handles information.

Information you provide

When you submit a reservation, delivery or enquiry form, you may provide your name, phone number, email address, delivery address, landmark and other details necessary to respond to your request.

Location

The food ordering page can request browser location to calculate approximate distance from Daisy and determine whether the location falls within the supplied 8 km delivery radius. Location access is controlled by your browser.

Analytics

GA4 is optional and configurable. If enabled, analytics events should avoid unnecessary personal information. UTM attribution may be stored in the browser session to understand campaign source and form context.

Third-party services

WhatsApp, Google Maps and any configured form endpoint are external services. Their own privacy policies and terms apply when you interact with them.

Data minimisation

Do not submit passwords, payment credentials or other sensitive information through this website. Server-side form handlers should validate, sanitise, rate-limit and protect submitted data.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Daisy at hello@daisycafeneatery.com.
