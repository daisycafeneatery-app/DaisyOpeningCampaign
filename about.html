Our story

A little place we created with a lot of heart.

Daisy began on 25th December, 2025, with a simple dream — to create a place in Debra where good food, beautiful moments and a warm sense of belonging could come together.

Daisy Cafe & Eatery

“We opened our doors with a dream. You turned it into a home.”

We wanted Daisy to feel different. Not just another café, but a place that feels cozy, warm and effortlessly sophisticated — a little escape from the everyday, right here in our neighbourhood.

From carefully prepared food and comforting cups of coffee to conversations that last a little longer, every detail at Daisy is shaped by hygiene, quality, freshness and care.

We believe food should not only taste good, but should also make you feel good. That is why we continue to explore a menu that brings together café favourites, comforting classics and thoughtfully chosen options for different tastes and moods.

But what truly makes Daisy special is you. Every visit, every kind word, every returning face and every recommendation becomes a part of Daisy’s story.
